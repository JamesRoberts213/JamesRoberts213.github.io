Project Overview

Traditional small-scale wind turbines suffer from mechanical wear and bearing failure when deployed in harsh remote locations. This project investigates whether vortex-induced vibrations (VIV) and mechanical aerofoil deflection can be harnessed as a solid-state, long-life alternative for micro-energy harvesting.

Key Engineering Deliverables

  • Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD): Modeled transient 2D von Kármán vortex shedding dynamics in ANSYS Fluent utilizing $k-\omega$ SST turbulence models with wall-refined inflation layers ($y^+ \approx 1.13$) across varying Reynolds numbers.
  • CAD & Rapid Prototyping: Designed modular cylindrical aerofoil assemblies in SolidWorks and manufactured physical test components using 3D printing (PLA) and CNC water-jet cutting (mild carbon steel).
  • Experimental Wind Tunnel Testing: Conducted low-speed wind tunnel evaluations up to 20 m/s using half-bridge strain gauge circuits coupled to a Micro-Measurements P3 strain indicator and oscilloscope to record dynamic deflection.
  • Novel Aerodynamic Amplifier Design: Developed a custom feedback geometry that enhanced aerodynamic coupling, achieving a 5.32x increase in structural force capture efficiency over baseline geometry.

Key Insights & Personal Takeaways

CFD vs Real-World Discrepancies

Discovered how structural damping and physical mounting rigidity shift actual lock-in frequencies compared to idealized 2D Fluent simulations, emphasizing the need for empirical validation.

Signal Noise in Strain Measurement

Mastered half-bridge Wheatstone bridge configurations and dynamic filtering techniques to isolate subtle aerodynamic deflections from ambient wind tunnel vibrations.

Rapid Prototyping Iterations

Gained hands-on experience balancing component weight, stiffness, and structural tolerances across subtractive CNC steel cutting and FDM 3D printing.

Technical Presentation Defense

Refined the ability to condense complex fluid dynamics derivations and experimental datasets into a clear, compelling 15-minute viva presentation for academic panel defense.
