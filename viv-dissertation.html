A rigorous, first-principles investigation combining analytical dynamic beam modeling, transient 2D Computational Fluid Dynamics (ANSYS Fluent), and experimental wind tunnel testing to evaluate solid-state kinetic energy conversion for ultra-low-power remote sensor architecture.

Executive Summary

Conventional micro-scale rotary wind turbines fail in off-grid sensor deployments due to mechanical bearing wear and rotational friction. This project evaluates solid-state energy extraction using vortex-induced vibrations (VIV) coupled with mechanical aerofoil deflection. By matching vortex shedding frequencies to the structural natural frequency of a cantilevered bluff body, aerodynamic force capture is maximized to produce scalable mechanical strain for kinetic harvesting.

Critical Engineering Insights

CFD vs. Empirical Lock-in Discrepancies

Quantified how physical support rigidity and structural damping shift the true lock-in envelope relative to idealized 2D Fluent numerical models, proving the necessity of empirical boundary calibration.

Strain Gauge DAQ Signal Conditioning

Configured half-bridge Wheatstone bridge circuits ($GF = 2.08$) and dynamic signal filtering to isolate micro-strain mechanical deflections from ambient wind tunnel motor noise.

Hybrid Subtractive/Additive Manufacturing

Optimized the stiffness-to-mass ratio by combining CNC waterjet-cut carbon steel flexure elements with FDM 3D-printed aerodynamic shell profiles.

Academic Defense & Data Synthesis

Distilled multi-variable CFD datasets, structural frequency equations, and experimental strain records into a concise, defense-ready 15-minute viva presentation.

Near-Wall Grid Sensitivity ($y^+ \approx 1.13$)

Evaluated the critical impact of boundary layer inflation layer resolution on correctly predicting pressure-driven separation points under transient $k-\omega$ SST formulations.

ULP Energy Conversion Trade-Offs

Demonstrated that broadening the structural resonant lock-in bandwidth yields superior operational reliability for remote sensor nodes compared to chasing narrow peak power output.
