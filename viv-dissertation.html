Executive Summary & Governing Aeroelastic Physics

Conventional micro-scale rotary wind turbines fail in off-grid sensor deployments due to mechanical bearing wear and rotational friction. This project evaluates solid-state energy extraction using vortex-induced vibrations (VIV) coupled with mechanical aerofoil deflection. By matching vortex shedding frequencies to the structural natural frequency of a cantilevered bluff body, aerodynamic force capture is maximized to produce scalable mechanical strain for kinetic harvesting.

1. Strouhal Shedding Lock-In

$$f_s = \frac{\text{St} \cdot U}{D} \approx f_n = 6.5\text{ Hz}$$

When incident velocity $U$ drives vortex shedding frequency $f_s$ into the structural natural frequency $f_n$, hydrodynamic lock-in amplifies transverse oscillation amplitude.

2. Euler-Bernoulli Cantilever Dynamics

$$m_{\text{eff}}\ddot{y} + c\dot{y} + ky = \frac{1}{2}\rho U^2 D L\, C_L(t)$$

Trailing-edge amplifier feedback boosts dynamic lift coefficient $C_L(t)$ by $5.32\times$, maximizing cantilevered root bending strain $\varepsilon_{\text{root}}(t)$.

Key Architectural Upgrades

  • Uniform 50 mm Diameter: Eliminates spanwise diameter tapers to lock in a single, coherent Strouhal shedding frequency ($f_s = \text{St}\cdot U / D = 6.5\text{ Hz}$) along the active span.
  • Hemispherical Dome Tip: Suppresses 3D free-end tip vortex downwash that otherwise disrupts spanwise vortex correlation near the upper tip.
  • Scaled 50 mm Amplifier Shroud: Matches the trailing-edge feedback shroud geometry directly to the 50 mm bluff body, retaining the 5.32x dynamic lift boost.
  • Decoupled Base Wake Sleeve: Connects the lower cylindrical fairing directly to the baseplate to establish a uniform, constant wake field while isolating the cantilevered load-bearing flexure from root turbulence.
  • 6.5 Hz Resonant Frequency Tuning: Engineered flexure stiffness and tip mass to align $f_n$ directly with the tunnel's peak lock-in velocity envelope ($6.5–7.0\text{ m/s}$).

Why Cantilever Decoupling Matters

In the initial prototype, mounting the entire bluff body directly to the strain-gauged flexure exposed the root to floor boundary-layer turbulence. By separating the lower fixed wake sleeve from the upper oscillating aerofoil via an internal metal flexure core, the upper body oscillates purely in the clean freestream while concentrating bending strain cleanly at the instrumented root.

Critical Engineering Insights

CFD vs. Empirical Lock-in Discrepancies

Quantified how physical support rigidity and structural damping shift the true lock-in envelope relative to idealized 2D Fluent numerical models, proving the necessity of empirical boundary calibration.

Strain Gauge DAQ Signal Conditioning

Configured half-bridge Wheatstone bridge circuits ($GF = 2.08$) and dynamic signal filtering to isolate micro-strain mechanical deflections from ambient wind tunnel motor noise.

Hybrid Subtractive/Additive Manufacturing

Optimized the stiffness-to-mass ratio by combining CNC waterjet-cut carbon steel flexure elements with FDM 3D-printed aerodynamic shell profiles.

Academic Defense & Data Synthesis

Distilled multi-variable CFD datasets, structural frequency equations, and experimental strain records into a concise, defense-ready 15-minute viva presentation.

Near-Wall Grid Sensitivity ($y^+ \approx 1.13$)

Evaluated the critical impact of boundary layer inflation layer resolution on correctly predicting pressure-driven separation points under transient $k-\omega$ SST formulations.

ULP Energy Conversion Trade-Offs

Demonstrated that broadening the structural resonant lock-in bandwidth yields superior operational reliability for remote sensor nodes compared to chasing narrow peak power output.
